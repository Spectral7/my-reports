The most dangerous scenarios often involve domains requiring ethical reasoning, nuanced interpersonal understanding, or decisions with significant human impact.

✅ Actionable Insights for Your Workflow

💡 Use AI as a Starting Point, Not Final Answer

Treat AI outputs as first drafts or research assistants. Always apply your own critical analysis, domain expertise, and judgment before acting on AI-generated content or recommendations.

💡 Define Clear Human Checkpoints

Establish explicit stages in your workflow where human review is mandatory—especially for decisions affecting people, finances, reputation, or ethics.

💡 Maintain Your Critical Thinking Skills

Resist the temptation to let AI do all the thinking. Regularly practice analysis, writing, and problem-solving without AI assistance to keep your skills sharp.

💡 Question AI Confidence

AI often presents information with unwarranted certainty. Develop the habit of asking: "What sources support this? What might be wrong here? What context is missing?"

💡 Leverage AI for Efficiency, Humans for Wisdom

Let AI handle volume, speed, and data processing. Reserve human attention for strategy, relationship decisions, creative direction, and ethical considerations.
